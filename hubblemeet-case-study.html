HubbleMeet

A professional networking app designed for entrepreneurs, founders, and professionals. Built to create meaningful connections and foster authentic professional relationships in a curated ecosystem.

3 months

Overview

Building authentic professional connections

HubbleMeet reimagines professional networking by focusing on quality over quantity. The app creates a curated space where entrepreneurs, founders, and professionals can build meaningful relationships rather than just collecting contacts.

The challenge was to design an experience that felt personal and intentional — moving away from the transactional nature of traditional networking platforms. The result is an interface that encourages authentic conversations and long-term professional relationships.

Outcome

Impact & learnings

HubbleMeet successfully launched with a focused user base of verified professionals. The clean, intentional design helped differentiate the platform from competitors and established a strong visual identity that resonates with the target audience.

Key learnings included the importance of progressive disclosure in complex networking flows, the balance between personality and professionalism in UI design, and how thoughtful microinteractions can make digital networking feel more human.
