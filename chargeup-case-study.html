OVERVIEW

Multi-stakeholder EV ecosystem

ChargeUp is a comprehensive platform that manages the complete lifecycle of EV batteries and vehicles while coordinating between financial institutions, field teams, and supply partners. The platform streamlines onboarding, asset tracking, financing workflows, service management, and operational reporting across six interconnected applications.

Platform Components: Driver Mobile App, Employee Mobile App, Dealer/Distributor Web Portal, NBFC Web Portal, OEM Web Portal, and CRM Web Portal — each designed for specific operational roles within the ecosystem.

KEY FEATURES

Designed for every stakeholder

Driver App: Battery health monitoring, EMI payment tracking, referral system, service ticket management, and battery analytics including charge trends and usage data.

Employee App: Lead management, KYC verification, EMI collection, asset lifecycle tracking (In Stock → Assigned → Recovery → Immobilized → Recovered), wallet management, and service ticket resolution.

Web Portals: Dealer inventory and service management, NBFC loan approvals and EMI monitoring, OEM product catalog and asset distribution tracking, CRM operational oversight and cross-team coordination.
