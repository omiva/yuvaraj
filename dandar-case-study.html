Redesigning a digital healthcare platform to improve access to medical services in rural India — integrating consultations, diagnostics, and pharmacy into one seamless experience.

OVERVIEW

Healthcare accessibility for rural communities

Dandar Health is a digital healthcare platform focused on rural India, particularly in Jharkhand. The redesign integrates telemedicine consultations, diagnostic services, and pharmacy access into a unified mobile experience — bridging the gap between patients and quality healthcare in underserved areas.
