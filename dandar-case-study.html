Dandar Health

Overview

Bridging the rural–urban healthcare gap

Dandar Health is a digital healthcare platform focused on expanding access to quality medical services in underserved and rural regions of India, particularly across districts in Jharkhand. The platform combines telemedicine, physical clinics, pharmacy services, and diagnostics into a single integrated healthcare ecosystem.

The patient app redesign aimed to simplify the fragmented healthcare journey — where patients traditionally visit multiple locations for consultations, tests, and medicines — into a unified digital experience that brings specialist care, diagnostics, and medications directly to their fingertips.

Outcome

Impact & learnings

The redesigned Dandar Health patient app successfully streamlined the healthcare journey for rural users by consolidating fragmented services into a single, intuitive platform. By integrating consultations, diagnostics, and pharmacy services digitally, the app reduced the need for patients to travel long distances to access specialist care.

Key learnings included understanding the unique constraints of rural healthcare delivery — such as limited internet connectivity and varying digital literacy — and designing with progressive disclosure and clear visual hierarchy to ensure accessibility for all users.
